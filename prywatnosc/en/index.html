AmouNTHILL Privacy Policy

Effective from 4 November 2026.

This policy explains what data the AmouNTHILL app processes, why, where it goes and what rights you have. We keep it plain wherever we can. One rule comes first: your finances are yours. Most data stays on your phone, and we send out only what a feature you use actually needs.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Dominik Lipiński. For anything about privacy, including exercising your rights, write to domlip12@icloud.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

Without signing in, the app runs entirely on your device. The database on your phone stores:

  • entries: expenses, income, savings, amounts, dates, categories, notes and shop names,
  • categories, limits, goals, recurring rules, fixed costs and calendar events,
  • savings jars, including the account type, bonds and ETFs with the number of units,
  • receipts stored as data (shop, items, amounts), not as photos,
  • app settings such as language, currency, billing cycle and reminder times.

We cannot see or access this data unless you turn on sync.

If system backup is turned on on your phone (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone), the system may include app data, including this database, in it. The backup is created and stored by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, not by us. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

3. Account and cloud sync

If you sign in with a Google account, your ledger syncs with the server so it works on several devices, survives a phone change and enables a shared budget.

  • What data: your Google account email and identifier, plus all ledger data listed in section 2.
  • Where: on Supabase servers in the European Union (Paris region).
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract, i.e. providing the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • How long: until you delete your account. Deleting the account in Settings removes your data from the server.

Google handles sign-in. When you sign in, Google gives us your email address and account identifier, and you can revoke access at any time in your Google account settings.

4. Shared budget

When you invite household members or join someone else's budget, all members see shared entries together with who added them (the name you provide). Entries and categories marked as private are visible only to their author. When the owner deletes their account, members receive a copy of the entries they added themselves.

5. AI features

Some features use Google's Gemini language model. Requests always go through our server, and the service key never reaches your phone.

  • Receipts, order screenshots and invoices: the text is recognised on your phone (Apple or Google on-device text recognition). We send the AI only the recognised text and the list of your categories, never the photo itself. The photo stays in temporary storage until the form closes, unless you save it to your gallery.
  • Dictated entries: when a category cannot be matched on the phone, we send the dictated text and the list of categories to the AI.
  • Chat with Mou (premium): only with your consent, when you ask Mou a question we send it together with a summary of your data: spending, income and savings totals for the last 6 months, spending totals by category, entries from this and last month (date, amount, category, note), recurring entries, savings jar balances, values and goals (for bonds and instruments also their type, number of units, price and gain), category names and limits, plus the last few messages of the conversation so Mou understands the context. We also send how Mou should address you (masculine, feminine or neutral forms) if you choose one. When you talk to Mou by voice, we send her answer once more to the same AI model so it can read it aloud; we do not store the recordings. We do not send private entries, shop names or receipt photos. You can withdraw consent in Settings. Your conversation history with Mou stays only on your phone, in the ledger data: conversations older than 90 days are deleted automatically, and you can delete a single conversation or the whole history yourself in the app. If you turn on “Help improve Mou” in Settings (off by default), we store on our server your questions, Mou's answers with the cards and changes she suggested, and whether you saved, edited or undid a change, without the data summary, to improve Mou. Exchanges older than 90 days are deleted the next time a conversation of yours is stored; turning the switch off deletes them immediately, and all of them are deleted together with your account. When you rate a Mou answer with a thumb, we store on our server the question, the answer, the rating and any comment (without the data summary) to improve Mou; we delete them together with your account.
  • Unknown broker report: only with your consent, we send a sample of the file (sheet names and first rows) with long numbers, email addresses and name fields masked. We keep the sample until we add support for your report.

Apart from rated Mou answers and conversations stored after you turn on “Help improve Mou”, we do not store the content of AI requests. We store only usage counters: day, task type, number of requests and errors, number of tokens used, seconds of speech recognition in conversations with Mou, and plan type. They are used to enforce free-plan limits and keep service costs in check, and we delete them together with your account. Google processes the text to generate a response under the Gemini API terms. We use the paid version of this service, in which Google does not use the submitted content to train or improve its models. To use AI without signing in, we create an anonymous technical account that holds no data about you.

The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for the chat with Mou and the report sample, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

6. Speech recognition

Dictation uses your phone's system speech recognition: Apple on iPhone, Google on Android. Depending on the device and language, audio may be processed on the phone or on Apple or Google servers, under their privacy policies. The app does not store recordings, only the resulting entry text.

In a premium conversation with Mou, with your consent, the sound of your question is streamed live to the ElevenLabs speech recognition service, which turns it into text. This only happens while Mou is listening to your question. Waiting for "hey Mou" happens on your phone. Without premium, over the limit or offline, the system speech recognition is used.

7. Phone payments

  • iPhone: if you set up an automation in the Shortcuts app, after an Apple Pay payment the system passes the amount, shop name and card name to the app. The data goes only to your ledger.
  • Android: if you grant notification access, the app reads only Google Wallet payment notifications. It does not open, store or send any other notifications.

You can turn this off at any time in your phone settings.

8. Files and reports you send yourself

You open or save broker reports, backups, CSV exports, photos and invoices yourself, through the system file picker or "Share". Broker reports are read on the phone. Backups and exports go wherever you save or send them and do not pass through our servers.

When you send "Report a bug" yourself, we store your description, app version, phone system and account ID on the server to fix the problem. We don't attach entries or other app data. Reports are deleted with your account.

9. Notifications, camera and other permissions

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on the phone. We do not send notifications from a server.
  • Camera: only for scanning receipts and the QR code of a shared-budget invitation.
  • Photos: only when you pick a photo yourself or save a receipt photo to your gallery.

10. Market data

The app downloads bond, inflation and ETF prices from public files on GitHub. As with any internet connection, GitHub sees the device's IP address. No data about your savings is sent. Current prices of the instruments in your savings jars are downloaded directly from Yahoo Finance: Yahoo then sees your device's IP address and the instrument's name, but no amounts.

11. Rewarded ads

On the free plan you can choose to watch an ad to get an extra receipt or unlock a feature for an hour. An ad never appears unless you tap to watch it, and the premium plan has no ads.

Ads are provided by Google (AdMob). The ad library only starts when you choose "Watch an ad" and, where required, give consent in the consent window. Google may then process device data, your IP address and ad display data, for example to deliver and measure the ad and to prevent abuse. The legal basis is your consent, which you can change or withdraw in Settings under "Ad consent".

Ads are not personalised. We don't pass entries, amounts, categories, your email address or account ID to Google, only a one-time number that confirms the ad was watched.

12. Usage statistics

To know which features are needed and what breaks, the app counts how often parts of it are used, for example how many entries were added by voice or how many times the premium offer was opened.

  • What data: a random install number, the day, the event name and its count, plus the app version, operating system and plan type.
  • What we don't collect: amounts, shop names, categories, notes, conversation content, your email address or account ID. We don't link the install number to your account.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in developing and fixing the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • How long: 13 months, after which the rows are deleted.
  • How to turn it off: in Settings, under “Usage statistics”. Turning it off deletes the number from your phone and the rows from the server. Without a connection, the rows stay on the server until those 13 months end, but they can no longer be linked to your phone.

13. What we don't do

  • We don't sell data or give advertisers information about your finances.
  • We don't use personalised ads, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.
  • We don't connect to your bank and never know your banking logins or passwords.

14. Recipients

Only providers needed to run the service process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and app server, servers in the EU),
  • Google (Google sign-in, Gemini model, speech recognition on Android, rewarded ads, where it also acts as an independent controller),
  • Apple (speech recognition on iPhone, Shortcuts automations),
  • ElevenLabs (speech recognition in premium conversations with Mou),
  • GitHub (market data files).

In addition, the app connects directly to Yahoo Finance to fetch prices of the instruments in your savings jars (section 10).

Some of these companies are based in the USA. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, this is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

15. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy,
  • rectify your data,
  • erase your data,
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability (CSV export and backup in Settings),
  • object to processing,
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing,
  • lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland) or the authority in your country.

You can do most of this yourself in the app: edit and delete entries, export data and delete your account. For anything else, write to domlip12@icloud.com. We reply within one month.

16. Children

The app is intended for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children.

17. Security

Connections to the server are encrypted. On the server, each ledger is separated by access rules, and keys to external services are kept only on the server. Data on your phone is protected first of all by your device's screen lock.

18. Changes

When we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you about significant changes in the app.
